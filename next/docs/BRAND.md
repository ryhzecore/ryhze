# Ryhze web identity

This implementation guide applies the decisions in **Ryhze Branding Questions**, **Ryhze - Planning**, **Game Design Document**, and the supplied **Ryhze — Brand Guidelines v2.0**. It accompanies the website; it does not replace the master brand guidelines.

## Character and voice

Comfort first. The experience should feel calm, trustworthy, cinematic, welcoming and quietly confident. Use clear, short language. Present real work honestly: concept artwork is labelled, unfinished titles have no invented release dates, and unavailable media has an explicit state.

The brand line is **Entertainment has no limits.** Ryhze remains the parent identity. Ryhze Studio identifies films, Ryhze Television identifies series, and Ryhze Games identifies games. These are production labels within one experience.

## Visual identity

- Use the supplied wordmark and symbol without redrawing, stretching, cropping or adding effects. Wordmark minimum 120 px; symbol minimum 32 px. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the symbol height.
- Primary violet: **#5500FF**. Use it in spatial lighting and large surfaces. Keep everyday controls neutral and legible.
- Supporting palette: **#BF70FF**, **#FF7979**, **#79E2FF**, **#FF79ED**. These are accents, not simultaneous competing themes.
- Foundations: black, charcoal **#171717**, white. The website uses a near-black canvas and translucent charcoal panels.
- **Space Grotesk** for headings and concise display copy; **Inter** for controls and reading. Fonts are hosted with the site.
- Use approximately 5% horizontal page insets, fluid type, and a restrained grid. Recompose on phones rather than shrinking a desktop canvas.

## Components and motion

Controls share a pill silhouette, centered transform origin, minimum 44 px touch targets and gentle 550 ms hover easing. Cards and panels use 20–28 px rounded corners. Glass uses subtle borders, translucency and blur without reducing text contrast.

Games and Films share a sliding selection pill. Title cards and hero artwork expand into detail panels through shared layout transitions, returning to the same source. Back and player controls sit outside the picture. Pointer interaction has no browser-blue ring; keyboard focus remains visible in white.

Smooth motion is the default requested for Ryhze. The account menu provides a persistent Reduced motion option. Audio is opt-in, fades between states and pauses while a title is open. Do not fabricate previews for titles without media.

## Content and access

The public catalogue contains Ryhze's original work. Larcenous Driftscape is in development, set in San Coronado, and uses concept world artwork explicitly marked as not gameplay. Legacy reference titles are confined to the authenticated internal catalogue and must not be described as Ryhze productions.

Membership is invitation-only. User-facing copy must never claim that the site is unhackable or that unfinished games are available to play.
