Privacy & cookies

Your account

Ryhze stores your user ID, a salted password hash, account status, and sign-in sessions in Cloudflare D1. Passwords are not stored as readable text. Administrators can invite or disable members.

Essential cookies

A secure, HTTP-only session cookie keeps you signed in. Normal sessions expire after 12 hours. Choosing “Remember me” keeps the session for up to 30 days. Signing out revokes the current session. These cookies are necessary to provide access; if you block them, sign-in cannot work.

Your preferences

Saved titles and your music preference are stored in this browser. Clearing site data removes them. They are not used for advertising.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare hosts the site and media and processes requests to deliver them. Login attempts are temporarily rate-limited to reduce password guessing. Expired sessions, invitations, and rate-limit records are removed daily. Ryhze does not add advertising trackers or collect precise location data.

Account requests

Contact your Ryhze administrator to request account access, correction, or deletion.
